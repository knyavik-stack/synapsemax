# SynapseMax DEX v3

## Why v3 exists

DEX v2 over-corrected toward simplicity. It reduced content density, weakened the business narrative and made the interface feel like a generic futuristic landing page.

DEX v3 restores the original direction: a serious B2B transformation platform expressed through a HUD/FUI visual language.

## Core narrative

**Сложность бизнеса → данные и процессы → Synapse → ИИ → автоматизация → управляемая система → бизнес-результат.**

The site must make the following proposition understandable without prior knowledge of SynapseMax:

> SynapseMax transforms fragmented business processes and data into an intelligent, automated and controllable digital system.

## Content architecture

1. Hero — what SynapseMax does and why it is different.
2. Отправная точка — why digital businesses still remain fragmented.
3. Решения — Automation + AI + Digital Transformation as one stack.
4. Подход — visible transformation from complexity to system.
5. Кейсы и сценарии — mechanics of operational, intelligence and management transformation.
6. Архитектура — intelligence layer, governance and business effect.
7. Contact — diagnostic-first CTA.

## Visual rules

- Canonical S symbol is never cut or redesigned.
- Synapse activity lives around the central junction and in the surrounding HUD environment.
- Use real semantic icons, not generic emoji or stock iconography.
- Neon is a signal layer, not the content itself.
- Keep information density high enough for a serious B2B platform.
- Russian section names are used where English adds no meaning.
- Wordmark uses the canonical asset; interface typography is separate from brand typography.
- Animation must explain state, flow or transformation.

## Future DEX v4

Only after visual approval of v3: interaction states, scroll-linked transformation, richer HUD telemetry, responsive motion tuning and AI transformation assessment.
